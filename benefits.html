Wellness first

Why cold-pressed oils make a real difference for your family.

Heavily refined oils go through heat, bleach, and chemical treatment that strips away the natural goodness. At Arumee, we keep things gentle — so what comes out of the press is exactly what goes into your kitchen.

Real benefits in every bottle

Our pressing method protects both nutrition and taste, so your food feels cleaner, richer, and more like it was always meant to.

Nutrients retained

Our low-temperature pressing keeps natural antioxidants and Vitamin E right where they should be — in your oil, not burned off in a refinery.

No artificial treatment

No bleaching. No deodorizing. No synthetic fragrances. Your oil stays as close to its natural source as possible — exactly how it should be.

Better flavour depth

Cold-pressed groundnut and gingelly oils carry rich, traditional aromas that make your everyday cooking taste the way it always should have.

Gentle processing

Slow pressing is kind to the oil. No aggressive industrial heat cycles, no shortcuts — just consistent quality and taste you can rely on, batch after batch.

Cleaner ingredient confidence

You know exactly what's in your bottle: pure oil. Nothing added to mask it, alter it, or make it last longer than it naturally would.

Fresh small-batch output

Small batches mean your oil isn't sitting in a warehouse for months. It arrives closer to the pressing date — fresh, aromatic, and full of life.
