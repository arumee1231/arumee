Frequently Asked Questions

Curious about our oils, how we press, or how to order? We've answered everything honestly below.

What is Arumee?

Arumee is a premium oil brand based in Namakkal, Tamil Nadu. We produce pure cold-pressed coconut oil and wooden-pressed (marachekku) groundnut & gingelly (sesame) oils at Mani Flour & Oil Mill. All oils are FSSAI certified (licence 22425133000137), free of chemicals and preservatives, and delivered free within Tamil Nadu.

Is Arumee oil truly cold-pressed?

Yes. All our oils are cold-pressed at low temperature — well below the threshold that destroys natural nutrients, antioxidants, and aroma. Our groundnut and gingelly oils are extracted using the traditional wooden marachekku press; our coconut oil is cold-pressed with no heat treatment, bleaching, or chemical solvents at any stage.

Why is cold-pressed oil better than refined oil?

Refined oils undergo high-heat processing, chemical bleaching, and deodorizing — stripping away natural vitamins, antioxidants, and flavour. Cold-pressed oils retain their full nutritional profile including Vitamin E, natural antioxidants like sesamol (in gingelly oil) and lauric acid (in coconut oil), along with authentic aroma and taste.

Is delivery free? Where do you deliver?

Yes, all Arumee orders include free delivery within Tamil Nadu. We currently deliver only within Tamil Nadu. We’ll expand to other states soon. We dispatch from our mill in Namakkal, Tamil Nadu. Most orders arrive within 3—5 business days.

Why can online delivery pricing differ from direct mill purchase pricing?

Online delivery pricing may differ from direct mill purchase pricing because it includes packaging, order handling, and transportation to your address. The total online amount covers these delivery-related costs for doorstep convenience.

What is marachekku oil?

Marachekku oil is cooking oil extracted using a traditional wooden press called a “marachekku” in Tamil. The slow, cold extraction preserves natural nutrients, aroma, and flavour without chemicals or high heat. Arumee’s groundnut and gingelly oils are made using this ancestral wooden press method at Mani Flour & Oil Mill in Namakkal, Tamil Nadu.

How should I store cold-pressed oil?

Store cold-pressed oil in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Keep the bottle tightly sealed after use. Avoid storing near a stove or window. Cold-pressed oils do not contain preservatives, so proper storage helps retain freshness, aroma, and nutritional value for longer.

What is the shelf life of cold-pressed oil?

Cold-pressed oils typically have a shelf life of 6 to 12 months when stored correctly in a cool, dark place. Because Arumee oils are pressed in small batches and shipped fresh from the mill, they arrive with maximum shelf life remaining. Always check the best-before date on your bottle.

Is cold-pressed coconut oil good for hair and skin?

Yes. Cold-pressed coconut oil retains its natural lauric acid, Vitamin E, and medium-chain fatty acids, making it excellent for hair conditioning, scalp massage, and skin moisturising. Because it is unrefined and chemical-free, it is gentler than refined coconut oil for personal care use.

Is cold-pressed groundnut oil suitable for deep frying?

Yes. Cold-pressed groundnut oil has a naturally high smoke point and is widely used for deep frying in South Indian cooking. Its rich, nutty flavour enhances the taste of fried foods. However, for best nutrition, cold-pressed oils are ideal for low to medium-heat cooking and tempering.

What is the difference between gingelly oil and sesame oil?

Gingelly oil and sesame oil are the same product — “gingelly” is the traditional South Indian name for sesame oil. Arumee’s gingelly oil is extracted from sesame seeds using a wooden marachekku press, retaining its deep aroma, sesamol antioxidants, and Vitamin E content. It is commonly used in Tamil cooking, pickles, and Ayurvedic practices.
