Our approach: curiosity, with a healthy dose of caution

Market Scout by Arumee is a place to read about companies across India, Singapore, China mainland, Hong Kong and the US. The aim is simple: make the news understandable, and leave room for your own judgement.

Start with the source.

Buddy sends the market letter to our API. We publish that message, including its direct source links, without inventing prices or filling gaps with made-up news.

Give the price a date.

A price without a currency and timestamp can mislead. Check the price date in each letter and verify the current quote with your broker or exchange.

Read the risk, too.

Good company news does not guarantee a rising share price. Penny shares can be especially volatile and difficult to sell. We keep the risk visible alongside the story.

Keep your own judgement.

This publication provides educational news context. It does not consider your financial situation, offer personalised advice, or promise investment returns.
